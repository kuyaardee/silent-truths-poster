Silent Truths Poster

A private publishing tool for the SilentTruths YouTube channel.

Silent Truths Poster is a small script that runs on the channel owner's own computer. Each day it uploads one original animated Short, written and produced by the owner, to the owner's own YouTube channel, SilentTruths, and confirms that the video is live.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the SilentTruths channel. It is not offered, sold or made available to anyone else, and it has no sign-up, no accounts and no other users.

What it does with YouTube

It uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read that channel's own list of uploads. It does not access other channels, other people's data, or any third-party content.
